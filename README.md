
# Axis Communications Axis Certified Professional Certification Exam Guide

A dedicated study guide and technical reference repository for professionals preparing for the **Axis Network Video Exam (ANVE-001)** and the Axis Certified Professional credential, covering network video technology, Axis products, system design, cybersecurity, and installation.

---

## Exam Overview

The Axis Network Video Exam validates advanced knowledge of network video surveillance technologies, Axis products, system design, and industry best practices.

- **Exam Format**: Computer-based, multiple-choice examination with single-answer and multiple-answer questions; available online and at Pearson VUE test centers.
- **Target Audience**: Network video system designers, technical sales professionals, system integrators, and video surveillance specialists.
- **Prerequisites**: Axis states that the certification is open to anyone seeking to demonstrate network video competence; no formal prerequisite is specified.

---

## Core Exam Domains & Knowledge Areas

### 1. Axis Product Knowledge

- Understand Axis product naming conventions and product categories.
- Identify camera types, features, capabilities, and typical use cases.
- Review extended video products and available accessories.

### 2. Camera Elements

- Understand camera optics, lenses, and imaging principles.
- Review photographic elements that affect image quality.
- Identify key camera components and their functions.

### 3. Electromagnetic Radiation

- Understand visible light, infrared light, and thermal radiation.
- Review color management, lighting technology, and camera features.
- Understand thermal imaging technologies and their applications.

### 4. Video Compression

- Understand video compression principles and available codecs.
- Review bitrate, bandwidth, and storage considerations.
- Identify factors that influence video bitrate and quality.

### 5. Networking and Cybersecurity

- Understand basic networking concepts used in network video systems.
- Review network communication and video transmission fundamentals.
- Understand cybersecurity measures that protect network video systems.

### 6. System Design

- Identify requirements and considerations for network video surveillance design.
- Understand operational requirements and system planning.
- Apply Axis system design tools when developing solutions.

### 7. Installation and Troubleshooting

- Understand installation tools, hardware, and software features.
- Review camera mounts, accessories, and focusing techniques.
- Identify common system issues, troubleshooting methods, and maintenance resources.

### 8. Integration

- Understand the role of video encoders in network video systems.
- Review video management software and system integration.
- Understand how analytics enhance network video solutions.
